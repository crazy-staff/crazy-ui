Everything on this page runs in real phone-sized frames (390 px): the menus open as bottom drawers, as they
do on a phone. Each variant is shown in light and dark; the ↺ button above a frame replays the opening
animation. **A** in each group is what crazy-hub ships today (round 7); **B** and **C** are alternatives.
Pick one per group (or mix), and the pick becomes the library default.

Already decided and built (not part of this round): drawers below `sm`, rows at least 48 px with
`text-base` and 20 px icons, a title on every drawer, touch-size controls inside drawers, focus returning to
the trigger, reduced motion.

## 1. Drawer rows

How the rows of a menu look in the drawer.

- **A — Plain.** Rows straight on the drawer, 4 px apart, the neutral hover; a line before the danger row.
- **B — Grouped.** One inset card with hairlines between the rows, like a settings list.
- **C — Icon tiles.** Each icon in a small squircle tile, labels in medium weight, slightly taller rows.

## 2. Picked option

The picked option in pickers (theme, language) — in drawers and in dropdowns alike. Tap rows to move the
pick. The desktop dropdowns are under the frames.

- **A — Soft tint.** The soft accent (lighter in a dropdown), a small check at the end.
- **B — Full tint.** The soft accent everywhere, medium weight, the check in a filled accent dot.
- **C — Outlined.** A light tint inside a thin accent border.

## 3. Floating menu button

The button that opens the main navigation on a phone, bottom right. Tap it to open the drawer.

- **A — Surface.** The surface colour, a border and the overlay shadow.
- **B — Accent.** Filled with the accent, with the soft glow.
- **C — Frosted.** Translucent with a backdrop blur, no shadow.

## Superellipse corners

Corners use CSS `corner-shape: superellipse(1.5)` — halfway between a circle and a squircle. Chromium draws
it natively; Firefox and Safari use the `hyperellipse` polyfill (`registerSquircles()`). Set
`--cui-corner-shape` to `round`, `squircle` or any `superellipse(k)` to change every corner at once.

## Smaller element, smaller radius

Chips use the smallest radius, cards and panels the largest. A small button never turns into a pill.

## Polyfill rules

The polyfill paints background, border and shadow from the computed style, so:

- no `box-shadow` on inputs and other replaced elements, and none on transparent backgrounds;
- outlines are borders, not `ring-*` or inset shadows;
- background, border and shadow transitions switch off under `[data-squircle-fallback]`.

Append `?fallback` to a showcase URL to see the polyfill in Chromium.

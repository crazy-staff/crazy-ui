```tsx
<Card.Root interactive>
  <Card.Header>
    <Card.Title>Infinite mana</Card.Title>
    <Card.Description>Keeps your mana pool topped up in combat.</Card.Description>
  </Card.Header>
  <Card.Footer>
    <Button size="sm">Enable</Button>
  </Card.Footer>
</Card.Root>
```

Parts: `Card.Root`, `Card.Header`, `Card.Title`, `Card.Description`, `Card.Body`, `Card.Footer`.

| Prop (Root) | Type | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `interactive` | `boolean` | `false` |

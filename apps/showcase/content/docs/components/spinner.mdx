## Examples

### Sizes

`sm` fits in buttons and small text, `md` (default) next to body text, `lg` for a whole panel.

<ComponentPreview name="spinner/sizes" />

### Tones

`accent` (default), `muted`, or `current` to take the text colour of its parent.

<ComponentPreview name="spinner/tones" />

### In a button

Disable the button while it works and put a small spinner with `tone="current"` before the label.

<ComponentPreview name="spinner/button" />

### With a message

For longer waits, say what is happening next to it.

<ComponentPreview name="spinner/status" />

When you know how far along the work is, use [Progress](/docs/components/progress) instead.

## Accessibility

- Renders `role="status"` with `aria-label` (“Loading” by default). Change `label` to say what loads.
- With `prefers-reduced-motion` it keeps turning, slowly, so it still shows that something happens.

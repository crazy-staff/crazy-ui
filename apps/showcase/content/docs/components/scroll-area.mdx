## Props

| Prop | Type | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `orientation` | `'vertical' \| 'horizontal' \| 'both'` | `'vertical'` |
| `fade` | `boolean` (fade the top and bottom edges) | `false` |
| `classNames` | `{ viewport, scrollbar }` | — |

Parts: `ScrollArea.Root`, `Viewport`, `Content`, `Scrollbar`, `Corner`.

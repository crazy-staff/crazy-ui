### Variants

## Accessibility

- The row is a `<div>` unless you render it as a link or button; then it is focusable and shows the focus ring.
- `ItemGroup` has `role="list"`; give each row (or its wrapper) `role="listitem"`.
- The icon tile is decorative (`aria-hidden`). Label controls in `actions` (e.g. `aria-label` on a switch).

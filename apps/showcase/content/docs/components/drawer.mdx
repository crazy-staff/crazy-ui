```tsx
<Drawer trigger={<Button>Open drawer</Button>} title="Warrior starter" footer={<Drawer.Close render={<Button />}>Spawn kit</Drawer.Close>}>
  …
</Drawer>
```

Use a [Sheet](/docs/components/sheet) on desktop and a Drawer on small screens when the content is the same.

Parts: `Drawer.Root` (`swipeDirection`, `snapPoints` from Base UI), `.Trigger`, `.Content` (`showHandle`), `.Title`,
`.Description`, `.Footer`, `.Close`.
